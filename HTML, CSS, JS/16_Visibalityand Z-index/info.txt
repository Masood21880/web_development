Visibility & Z-Index
display: none;
Hides the element completely. It takes up no space on the page, and other elements move up to fill the gap.

visibility: hidden;
Hides the element visually, but preserves its space. The layout remains unchanged, leaving an empty box.

z-index
Controls the stacking order of overlapping elements along the Z-axis (front-to-back).

Note: Requires a position property (such as relative, absolute, or fixed) to take effect.

Higher values appear in front of lower values (e.g., z-index: 10 sits above z-index: 1).
